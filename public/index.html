How we roast

From the farm to your door in four steps

  1. 01

    Source

    Green coffee bought directly from three farms we visit, above market price.

  2. 02

    Roast

    Every Tuesday, in 12 kg batches, with a profile tuned for each origin.

  3. 03

    Rest

    48 hours of rest, so the gas escapes and the flavour settles.

  4. 04

    Ship

    Compostable bags with the roast date printed on the front.

Brew it at home

Four minutes with a Chemex

  1. 01

    Grind

    30 g of coffee, medium-coarse, about the size of sea salt.

  2. 02

    Let it bloom

    Wet the grounds with 60 g of water just off the boil and wait 30 seconds.

  3. 03

    Pour slowly

    Small circles up to 500 g of water, then let it drain.

When will my coffee arrive?

We roast on Tuesday and ship on Wednesday. Most orders arrive by Friday.

Whole bean or ground?

Whole bean keeps its flavour longer. If you don't have a grinder, we grind it for your brewing method.

Can I pause my subscription?

Yes. Pause, skip a month or cancel anytime, with no fee.

Are the bags recyclable?

They are home-compostable, including the valve and the label.
